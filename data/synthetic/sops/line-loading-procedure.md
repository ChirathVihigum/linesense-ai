# Line Loading Procedure (Katunayake)

> Synthetic demonstration document — not an official factory procedure.

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure governs how orders are loaded onto sewing lines Line 1 through Line 6 at the Katunayake plant. It is a factory-specific supplement to the Capacity Planning Rules and applies whenever a planner or supervisor assigns an order's remaining units to a line and shift.

## Capacity Check

Before loading, the planner confirms the target shift slot has enough remaining standard minutes for the order: allocated standard minutes must not exceed the slot's available operator minutes multiplied by its planned efficiency. Loading an order that would exceed a slot's remaining capacity is not permitted; the excess units remain unscheduled until additional capacity is available.

## Skill Compatibility

A line may only be loaded with a style whose required operations match the skills certified on that line's operator roster. As of this procedure's last review, Line 6 has no operator certified in buttonhole (BH); any style whose operation sequence includes a buttonhole operation must not be loaded onto Line 6 until an operator is cross-trained or reassigned from another line.

## Sequencing and Due Date

Where multiple orders compete for the same line and shift, orders are loaded in earliest-due-date order, with ties broken by order reference so the sequence is always reproducible. A supervisor may not reorder the queue informally without recording the reason, since doing so can create a due-date risk for the order pushed back.

## Confirmation

Once a line is loaded, the supervisor confirms the assignment on the shift board so the material handler and quality team know which order and style to expect; an unconfirmed load is treated as provisional and must not trigger material issue to the line.

## Related Procedures and Review

This procedure is read together with Skill Matrix Governance, which is the authoritative source for which skills each line currently has certified, and with Capacity Planning Rules, which defines the standard-minutes arithmetic used to check a slot's remaining capacity. The Line 6 buttonhole gap noted here is reviewed monthly against the current skill matrix and is expected to close once a certified operator is cross-trained; until then, planners must check this constraint explicitly rather than assume every line can run every style.
