# Shift Calendar and Breaks (Biyagama)

> Synthetic demonstration document — not an official factory procedure.

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines the shift calendar, shift codes, and scheduled breaks for lines Line B1 through Line B3 at the Biyagama plant, all times expressed in the plant's local timezone, Asia/Colombo.

## Shift Codes

Biyagama runs two shifts per working day, identified as shift code A and shift code B in every capacity slot record. Shift A runs the morning and early afternoon block; shift B covers the late afternoon and evening block. A capacity slot is always keyed by line, date, and shift code together, never by line and date alone.

## Scheduled Breaks

Each shift includes one meal break and two short tea breaks, all of which are already netted out of the available operator minutes recorded for that shift's capacity slot before any planning calculation runs. Planners must never apply a second break deduction on top of the recorded available operator minutes.

## Working Calendar

The Biyagama working calendar follows the national public holiday list plus two additional plant maintenance days per year, both published at the start of the financial year. A capacity slot is not created for a non-working day; an order due on a non-working day is treated as due at the start of the next working day for risk-escalation purposes.

## Access Note

This procedure is specific to the Biyagama plant's operating calendar and is not applicable to Katunayake, which maintains its own shift calendar; Katunayake users should not rely on this document for their plant's break timings.

## Related Procedures and Review

This procedure is the Biyagama-specific counterpart to the shift calendar Katunayake maintains separately; the two are never assumed to be identical, since public holiday observance and plant maintenance days can differ between the two locations even within the same organization. Capacity Planning Rules and Due-Date Risk Escalation both rely on the working calendar this procedure defines when computing available standard minutes and projected completion dates for Biyagama orders, so any calendar change here is communicated to the planning team before the next planning cycle begins.
