# Fabric Receiving and Inspection

> Synthetic demonstration document — not an official factory procedure.

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure governs the receipt and physical inspection of piece-goods fabric (materials M01 Cotton Pique Fabric, M02 Cotton Jersey Fabric, M03 Polyester Mesh Fabric, M04 Denim Twill Fabric, M05 Rib Knit Collar Fabric, and M06 Fusible Interlining) at both the Katunayake (KTN) and Biyagama (BYG) plants. It applies to every fabric lot arriving under a purchase order reference.

## Receiving Procedure

On arrival, the storekeeper records the delivery note, supplier lot code, and quantity in the intake register before the fabric is moved into the quarantine area. Every incoming lot is created with status QUARANTINE and may not be issued to any line, nor counted toward available stock, until it has passed inspection.

## Inspection Sampling

A four-point fabric inspection is performed on a sample of 5% of the rolls in each lot, rounded up to the next whole roll, selected from the top and middle layers of the pallet. Each sampled roll is inspected for shade variation (defect code DEF-SV), stains (DEF-ST), and physical width/weight tolerance.

## Acceptance Criteria

A lot passes inspection when its weighted four-point score across all sampled rolls is at or below the mill-agreed threshold and no sampled roll shows a critical safety defect such as metal contamination (DEF-MC). Lots that fail are marked REJECTED and returned to the supplier; lots that pass are marked ACCEPTED.

## Documentation

The inspection result, sampled roll numbers, and four-point scores are attached to the lot record before its status changes out of QUARANTINE, so the basis for acceptance can be reviewed later if a downstream defect is traced back to the lot.

## Related Procedures and Review

This procedure works together with Material Lot Acceptance, which governs the formal status transitions once inspection is complete, and with Material Reservation Policy, which explains why only ACCEPTED lots ever contribute to a material's available balance. This version's 5% sampling rate is superseded by a later revision that increases the sample to 10% of rolls per lot after an internal audit found the 5% rate occasionally missed shade variation concentrated in a small part of a lot; production planned or in progress under this version at the time of the change is not retroactively re-inspected, but every lot inspected from the revision's effective date onward must follow the current active version.
