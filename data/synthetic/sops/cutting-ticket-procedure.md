# Cutting Ticket Procedure

> Synthetic demonstration document — not an official factory procedure.

## Purpose and Scope

A cutting ticket authorizes the cutting department to draw a specific fabric lot and quantity against a specific order and lay plan. This procedure covers ticket creation, lay planning, and the handoff of cut components to the sewing lines at both KTN and BYG.

## Ticket Creation

The planner or supervisor creates a cutting ticket referencing the order reference (for example PO-KTN-0034), the style code (for example ST-03), and the fabric lots reserved for that order. A ticket is never created against a lot that is not ACCEPTED, and never for a quantity exceeding the order's active material reservation.

## Lay Planning

The cutting supervisor plans the marker and lay height to minimise fabric wastage while respecting the wastage fraction already built into the order's gross demand; lay planning that would require materially more fabric than reserved is escalated to the planner before cutting begins rather than drawn from another order's lot.

## Component Handoff

Cut components are bundled per size and colour, labelled with the order reference and style code, and handed off to the sewing line named on the cutting ticket. A bundle without a legible order reference is held at the cutting table until it can be re-labelled, since an unlabelled bundle risks being sewn against the wrong order.

## Reconciliation

At the end of each cutting run, actual fabric consumed is reconciled against the ticket's planned consumption; a variance beyond the approved wastage fraction is reported to the storekeeper the same day so the order's material reservation and projected balance stay accurate for downstream planning.

## Related Procedures and Review

This procedure hands off directly into Material Issue to Line for any trims the cut components require, and its wastage reconciliation feeds back into Reorder Point Policy through updated consumption history. A lay plan that would require materially more fabric than the order's active reservation is never approved informally; it is escalated to the planner, who may either adjust the reservation, following Material Reservation Policy, or revise the lay to fit within the approved wastage fraction. Cutting supervisors are reviewed annually on marker efficiency as part of the plant's IE performance review.
