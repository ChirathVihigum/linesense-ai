# Time Study Procedure

> Synthetic demonstration document — not an official factory procedure.

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines how the IE engineer records cycle-time observations used to set or revise a style operation's SAM value, at both KTN and BYG.

## Observation Sampling

A time study records at least ten observed cycles per operation, each as an observed time in seconds for one operator completing one unit of that operation, under normal working conditions rather than during a changeover or a known disruption. Fewer than ten observations may only be used as a provisional estimate, explicitly labelled as such.

## Recording

Each observation is recorded against the specific line, style, operation, and operator alias observed, together with the observation timestamp; observations are never recorded in aggregate only, since aggregate figures cannot later be checked for outliers.

## Deriving SAM

The representative single-operator time for an operation is derived from the observed cycles after outliers are handled per the Cycle-Time Outlier Handling procedure, then adjusted by an approved allowance factor to produce the SAM value maintained under the SAM Definition and Maintenance procedure.

## Re-Study Triggers

An operation is re-studied whenever its method, machine, or attachment changes, or whenever bottleneck escalation repeatedly identifies the same operation as the line's constraint despite the line otherwise appearing balanced, since this can indicate the operation's SAM no longer matches how it is actually performed.

## Related Procedures and Review

Time study output feeds the SAM Definition and Maintenance procedure directly, and any observation flagged during a study follows the Cycle-Time Outlier Handling procedure before it can influence a SAM value. New IE engineers are trained on this procedure using a shared reference operation so their sampling technique can be checked for consistency against a senior engineer's readings before they conduct an independent study. Time study records are retained for at least two style seasons so a later SAM dispute can be checked against the original observations. A study is never conducted by the same person who will personally benefit from its outcome, such as an operator being timed on their own rate-setting request, to keep the resulting SAM values credible to every line they will later apply to.
